Understand the Risk Levels

Risk depends on the type of information, the context, and what could happen if it is exposed.

🟢 LOW RISK

Usually non-sensitive information such as general educational questions or ordinary objects.

🟡 MODERATE RISK

Information that may involve privacy, personal context, academic-integrity or accuracy concerns.

🔴 HIGH RISK

Highly sensitive information such as OTPs, passwords, bank details, identity documents or biometrics.

🔐 Privacy Tips

Small habits can make AI use safer.

🔑 Never share passwords or OTPs.
AI tools do not need your authentication secrets.
🪪 Protect identity documents.
Avoid uploading Aadhaar, IDs or similar documents unless absolutely necessary and appropriate.
💳 Keep financial information private.
Do not share bank, card or payment credentials.
📍 Be careful with location.
Avoid sharing your exact live location unnecessarily.
📸 Think before uploading photos.
Check whether a photo reveals private information.
🎓 Check college rules.
Understand your institution's policy before using AI for assignments.
🔎 Verify important answers.
AI can make mistakes, so check important information.
🛡️ Use strong, unique passwords.
Never reuse sensitive passwords across important accounts.

🔑 Strong Password vs Weak Password

❌ Weak examples

password123
12345678
yourname123

These are examples for education—do not use them.

✅ Better approach

Use a long, unique passphrase that does not contain easily guessed personal information. Consider a reputable password manager.

💡 Remember

A password should be unique to the account. Never give your password or OTP to another person or an AI tool.

📸 AI Images & Social Media Privacy

AI-generated and edited photo trends can be fun, but think about what the original image reveals. Avoid uploading private photographs, school IDs, addresses, identity documents or other sensitive material to tools unless you understand the privacy implications.

👣 Understand Your Digital Footprint

Your digital footprint is the trail of information created when you use websites, apps, social media and online services.

🖱️ Active footprint

Information you deliberately share, such as posts, comments, photos, reviews and profile details.

⚙️ Passive footprint

Information that may be collected as you browse or use services, such as approximate location, device data, cookies and activity records.

🔎 Think before posting

Posts can be copied, screenshotted or shared beyond the audience you intended. Privacy settings reduce exposure but cannot guarantee control.

🎭 AI Images, Deepfakes & Voice Cloning

AI can generate or alter images, videos and audio. Convincing media is not automatically authentic, and visual clues alone may not prove whether it is genuine.

🖼️ AI-generated images

Images created from prompts or altered using AI may depict scenes or events that never happened.

🎬 Deepfake videos

Media that uses AI to imitate or alter a person's face, expressions or actions. It can be used creatively or to mislead.

🎙️ Voice cloning

AI-generated audio can imitate a person's voice. Do not rely on a familiar-sounding voice alone to verify an urgent request.

How to verify suspicious media

  • Check trusted, independent sources for the same event.
  • Look for the original source, date and context.
  • Contact the person through a separate, already-known channel if a request sounds unusual.
  • Do not forward unverified media or accuse someone based only on an AI detector.
  • Ask permission before uploading or editing another person's image or voice.

Remember: AI detectors can make mistakes. Treat results as clues, not proof.

🧭 What to Do After a Privacy Incident

Practical next steps depend on what happened.

Suspicious link or message

Do not click, reply, download attachments or share verification codes. Verify the sender separately.

Account may be compromised

Use the official app or website to secure the account, change its password, enable multi-factor authentication and review active sessions.

Money lost to a scam

Contact your bank or payment provider through an official channel, report promptly through 1930 and the official cybercrime portal, and preserve relevant records.

Private information shared

Remove the post or revoke access if possible, review settings, notify the service and seek trusted support if you feel unsafe.

Impersonation or manipulated media

Save relevant links or screenshots where safe, report the content on the platform and avoid resharing it.

Someone else's image or voice

Get permission before sharing or editing it. If content is used to harass or deceive, support the affected person and report it.
